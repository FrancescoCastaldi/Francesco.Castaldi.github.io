# Styleguide - Francesco Castaldi

## Design Concept
Space-themed tech personal branding landing page. Dark mode by default, high contrast typography, elegant and highly technical.

## Components
- **Header**: Sticky glassmorphism header with logo on the left and navigation/language toggle on the right.
- **Hero**: Imposing DM Serif heading, Inter subtitle, primary CTA (Star Gold), secondary CTA (transparent with border). Background uses CSS radial gradients and floating particles (`.hero-bg`).
- **Expertise Card**: Dark surface (`#0C111A`), hover elevates to `#131B27` and brightens border to Nebula or Gold based on the specific node color.
- **Node Colors**:
  - Healthcare: `#F59E0B`
  - Data Science: `#22D3EE`
  - Cloud Architecture: `#A78BFA`
  - Cybersecurity: `#34D399`
